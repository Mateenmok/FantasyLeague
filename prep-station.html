PREP STATION GUIDE

HOW TO USE IT

Use Calculator, Team Builder, and the live Note Sheet together so you can prep beforehand and still take clean in-match notes.

1. CALCULATE2. BUILD3. SCOUT
01
MATCHUP LAB

CALCULATOR

Use this page to compare two Pokémon, optimize SPs, and see live damage.

1Choose your Pokémon and the opponent. The side rosters are ordered by Speed and can be scrolled independently.
2Review the loaded set. Nature, Ability, Held Item, and four damaging moves are filled automatically. Calculator move menus stay damage-only unless a status move was loaded from Team Builder.
3Set the matchup. Adjust SPs, HP, status, forms/Megas, weather, terrain, Tailwind, screens, Helping Hand, and the other battle controls you need.
4Use the Optimization Center. Optimize normal Speed, a max-investment neutral-Nature Speed benchmark, minimum Speed for Trick Room, build styles, a guaranteed KO, or survival against a specific opponent move. The benchmark modes use the current Calculator battle state.
5Use Popular Spreads as a shortcut. Load a common spread instantly, then adjust it for the exact matchup you are testing.
COPY TO TEAM BUILDER → Adds your current left-side set to your six.
SEND TO NOTE SHEET → Sends the current opponent Pokémon only; revealed information stays blank.

02
YOUR SIX

TEAM BUILDER

Use this page to assemble and edit the six Pokémon you plan to bring.

1Add Pokémon manually or copy them from the Calculator. Each slot keeps its own editable set.
2Fine-tune every slot. Change forms/Megas, Nature, Ability, Held Item, SP spread, and all four moves. Team Builder shows the full Champions learnset, including status moves.
3Check the final stats. The card updates the finished stat numbers and SP total as you edit.
4Load a set in the Calculator. Use LOAD IN CALCULATOR when you want to test that exact build in a matchup.
5Status moves are fully supported here. If a saved set has one, loading that set into Calculator keeps it visible even though Calculator menus normally show damaging moves only.
QUICK REMINDERS

Best use: finalize your six here, keep full movesets intact, then load any slot into Calculator for matchup testing.
03
LIVE SCOUTING

NOTE SHEET

Use this page during a match to record what the opponent actually reveals.

1During team preview, send the opponent’s six from Calculator. Each click adds that Pokémon/form to the first empty slot with Ability, Item, moves, and notes intentionally blank.
2Fill in information as you see it during the battle. Record the revealed Ability, Held Item, and up to four moves.
3Use Pokémon Notes for that specific slot. Track things like Speed order, damage, role, targeting, or anything unusual about that Pokémon.
4Use Open in Calc mid-match when you need numbers fast. It loads that opponent exactly as recorded—unknown items, abilities, and move slots stay blank—so the Calculator never invents unrevealed information.
5Keep revealed info attached to that Pokémon. Item, Ability, moves, and Pokémon Notes stay with the slot so you can jump between scouting and calculations quickly.

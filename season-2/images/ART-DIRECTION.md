# Season 2 — highlands interface experiment

These assets and styles are scoped to `season-2/`. Season 1 keeps its existing branding and interface.

- `season-two-mascot.png`: the user's supplied transparent mascot, copied without alteration. Used for the Season 2 header, favicon, and trainer card.
- `highlands-dawn.png`: original landscape generated with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool. The full-resolution source is preserved; CSS adds background blur and contrast overlays.
- Existing trainer sprites and recoloring masks are retained so saved trainer appearances remain compatible.

## Final landscape prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: high-resolution widescreen landscape backdrop for the PokeLeague Season 2 trainer setup website. Create a pristine 16:9 atmospheric landscape inspired by the serene painterly, cel-shaded natural world of Pokémon Legends: Arceus. Rolling moss-green meadow, layered pine forests at the edges, distant blue slate mountains and one majestic snow-tipped peak toward the right third, soft cloud banks and pale turquoise sky, drifting low morning mist. Elegant muted teal, sage, olive and warm ivory palette. Sophisticated high-definition game-environment concept art, lush but restrained, subtle hand-painted texture, atmospheric depth, soft sunlight, tranquil sense of a new journey. Wide cinematic composition with quiet open central and left regions behind website text and menus; interesting landscape silhouette around the edges. Landscape only: no people, no creatures, no buildings, no logos, no typography, no interface, no frames, no watermarks. It will be softly blurred behind translucent charcoal interface panels; keep forms readable and clean, avoid busy tiny details.
